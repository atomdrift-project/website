---

By popular request, today we released **[Atomdrift Scan](/scan/) v2.9.0, which now ships with a [stable HTTP API](https://github.com/atomdrift-project/scan/blob/main/docs/SERVER_API.md). But that's not all! We also are announcing [Beamline](https://github.com/atomdrift-project/beamline/), our end-user low-latency API surface, which runs as a Cloudflare Worker.

Why go to all this trouble? Because users wanted something more than just a CLI tool.

This meant a couple of things had to happen:

- We had to plumb bearer tokens in anywhere
- We had to design a friendly JSON API
- We had to lower latency everywhere to make it reach our goal of <4s uncached analysis

So why the extra Beamline layer? Edge caching. Cloudflare Workers are embarassingly cheap to run, particularly if you use their NodeJS workers and cache. The idea is: you point end-users to Beamline; and then you run scan servers wherever you want. Beamline will handle the load-balancing and caching, ensuring most responses arrive back in <50ms.

We also upgraded our other tools to support the new scan release:

**[cleave](/cleave/) v2.9.0** preserves trait suppression and interdependency data
for modelling malecules and providing additional context to LLMs.

**[filefacts](/filefacts/) v1.5.0** offers better improved PE data, especially for handling expired certs.

**[stng](/stng/) v1.10.0** now recoves more XOR-encoded data.

**[fletch](https://github.com/atomdrift-project/fletch) v1.2.0** offers improved PURL handling, particularly for PURLs that ship with multiple files underneath them.
