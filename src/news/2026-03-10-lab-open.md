title: "Atomdrift Lab is now open"
date: 2026-03-10
summary: "Free public malware analysis sandbox for researchers and defenders."
---

[Atomdrift Lab](https://lab.atomdrift.org/) is now open for public use.
Submit files for automated atomic decomposition — no account required.

The lab runs the full atomdrift pipeline: stng extracts and decodes
strings, cleave performs deep static analysis across the binary and any
embedded source, and the results are presented in a structured report.

This is a free service for researchers, defenders, and anyone curious
about what's inside a binary. There are no tracking pixels, no
analytics, and no data sharing.

[Open the Lab &rarr;](https://lab.atomdrift.org/)
