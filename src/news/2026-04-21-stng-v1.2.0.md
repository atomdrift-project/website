title: "stng v1.2.0"
date: 2026-04-21
summary: "Preserve Telegram tokens, JWTs, and Swift mangled symbols; cut XOR false positives in binary data tables."
---

The chaos filter that drops obvious garbage was a little too eager.

v1.2.0 teaches it to keep three things it had been throwing out: Telegram bot tokens (`<bot_id>:<auth_token>`), JWTs — now gated on the `eyJ` header, so random `foo.bar.baz`-shaped Go symbols stop matching — and Swift mangled symbols (`_Tt`, `_T0`, `_$s`, `$s`).

XOR IP extraction also got more skeptical. A nine-byte slice of a SQLite VDBE opcode table will, under the right key, decode to something that looks exactly like an IP. The new heuristic checks the surrounding window for control-byte density: real ciphertext lives in varied data; a B-tree index page does not.
