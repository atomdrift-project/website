[stng](/stng/) v1.1.8 is a performance release that also closes a couple of coverage gaps.

XOR and string classification moved from linear scanning to Aho-Corasick. Disassembly is parallelized through iced-x86 and rayon. The SHA and rizin caches were tightened to avoid repeating work.

On coverage: XOR detection on ELF improved, and a PE/XOR bug that was mangling `office_update`-style samples is fixed.

Full notes on [Codeberg](https://codeberg.org/atomdrift/stng/releases/tag/v1.1.8).
